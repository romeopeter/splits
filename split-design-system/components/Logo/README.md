The Split logo: an S sliced by a playhead. The left pieces are ink and the right pieces slide up in blue, with the playhead in the cut. In the wordmark the sliced S is the first letter and the dot of the i is blue.

**Provide:** `variant` (`wordmark` | `mark` | `app-icon`) and `height`.

**Use:**
- The inline Logo takes its ink from `text-primary` and its blue from `text-link`, so it is right in both themes. For exported files use the SVGs in Logos (`on-dark`, `on-light`).
- Keep clear space of one playhead-head width on all sides. Minimum height: wordmark 20px, mark 16px.
- On photographs or colour fills, use the app icon or a one-colour white mark.

**Don't:** recolour the halves, straighten the slide, add a gradient, or set the name in another face. Redraw nothing: copy the SVG.
