The pointer and name tag of another person editing the same project, drawn over the canvas.

**Provide:** `name`, `color` (a `collab-*` token), and position it absolutely with `style`.

**Use:** keep the tag at 13px/500 on the collaborator fill with `text-on-accent`; fade it out after 4 seconds of no movement; never let it cover the playhead or selected handles.

**Don't:** use a collaborator colour for status; the colour identifies a person only.
